## MY TRAVEL AIGENT
We are building a custom agent with Gemini 3 (and the Google Cloud Agent Builder) to solve the real-world challenge of planning a trip, integrating with a partner's Model Context Protocol (MCP) server to give our agent the necessary abilities to do the job.

## Choosing a Partner
For the My Travel Aigent project, MongoDB is the ideal choice for our agent's database and persistent memory layer.
